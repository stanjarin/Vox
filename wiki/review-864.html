Cut (cards)

In card games, to cut the cards is to divide a deck into packets and reassemble it. The procedure usually follows shuffling and precedes dealing.

Purpose ✎

Cutting is traditionally used to reduce suspicion about a dealer's knowledge of the cards. It may also determine seating order or the first dealer.

⌃ Probabilities ✎

CutsPossible sequencesApprox.

For a standard deck of 52 cards, there are 51 possible cut points. The number of possible sequences increases exponentially with each successive cut.

These figures count the ordered series of cuts. Different sequences may lead to the same final arrangement, since cutting preserves the cyclic order of the cards.
