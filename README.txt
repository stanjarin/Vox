VOX covert-input prototype V4.
V4 changes only:
- Proper padlock icon at top.
- Fine hairline around passcode echo field.
- Echo accumulates left-to-right after each lock (e.g. K → K S → K S A → K S A D).
- Selection requires a short ~240 ms press-and-hold; ordinary taps do not lock.
- Suit phases always open on C (C-H-S-D).
Preserved from V3:
- Value phase initially opens on 9.
- Continuous wheel drag, momentum/deceleration, snap, and top/bottom rubber-band limits.
- 0 delay route after Card 1.
- Emergency hard reset.
